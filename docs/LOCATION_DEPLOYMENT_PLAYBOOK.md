## 2. Municipal Public Parks Playbook

### Why Parks Are High-Value Opportunities:
Parks frequently suffer from a lack of food and beverage infrastructure. Concession stands are notoriously seasonal, expensive to staff, and closed by 5:00 PM. A RoboPod provides park-goers with clean, cold, healthy snacks and drinks without requiring municipal staff.

### Implementation Checklist:
1. **Concession Licensing**:
   - Apply under Municipal Parks & Rec "Smart Park / Autonomous Concession" pilot tenders.
   - Position RoboPod as an amenity that enhances park safety (integrated 360° lighting, security cameras, emergency intercom).
2. **Utility Strategy**:
   - Connect to local park lighting panel (240V / 50A connection).
   - Rooftop 3.2 kW solar array provides daytime energy harvesting, reducing net grid draw by 35–45%.
3. **Aesthetic Compliance**:
   - Wrap container in municipal-approved anti-graffiti vinyl (e.g., wood-slat effect, park green, or public art mural).

## 3. Sports Stadiums & Event Arenas Playbook

### Why Stadium Grounds Are High-Value Opportunities:
Event venues experience massive surges. Inside stadiums, concessions charge $8 for a soda and have 20-minute lines. As 30,000–60,000 fans exit after an event at 10:30 PM, nearby convenience stores are instantly overwhelmed or closed.

### Implementation Checklist:
1. **Surge-Capacity Optimization**:
   - Leverage the **Mobile App 5-Second QR Pickup**: Fans leaving the stadium order on their phones during the final 5 minutes of the game, walk to the pod, scan their QR code, and pick up cold drinks and snacks without waiting.
   - Activate both Dispense Hatches (A and B) simultaneously to achieve **120 dispatches per hour**.
2. **Dynamic Surge Inventory**:
   - Configure planogram with 60% cold beverages (Pepsi, Gatorade, water, energy drinks) and 40% rapid snacks.
   - Implement event-day fast restocking schedule (replenishment van restocks 2 hours prior to gates opening).

## 4. Downtown High-Density Street & Parking Lot Playbook

### Why Downtown Sites Are High-Value Opportunities:
Traditional downtown convenience stores pay high retail rent ($8,000–$15,000/month) and face high labor costs and late-night security risks. RoboPod can occupy a single private parking space in a paid parking lot or curbside cutout for just $1,000–$1,500/month.

### Implementation Checklist:
1. **Site Selection**:
   - Partner with major commercial parking operators (e.g., Impark, Indigo, REEF Technology, SP+).
   - Choose well-lit corner stalls with high pedestrian sidewalk visibility.
2. **Anti-Vandalism & 24/7 Safety**:
   - Heavy-duty IK10 vandal-proof touchscreens with anti-glare coating.
   - High-intensity motion-sensor floodlighting illuminates an 8-meter perimeter around the pod, making customers feel safe at 2:00 AM.
   - Two-way audio/video intercom with 24/7 central monitoring station connection.
3. **Winterization & Extreme Weather (Canada & Northern US)**:
   - Heated hatch frames prevent snow and ice buildup from freezing the motorized access doors.
   - Heavy-duty internal HVAC keeps dairy and drinks at strict 3°C even during severe -30°C winter freezes.

## 5. Hub-and-Spoke Fleet Operations Model

For a city deploying a cluster of **5 to 10 RoboPods** (2 in major parks, 2 near stadiums/arenas, and 4 in downtown corridors):
- **1 Replenishment Van (Refrigerated Ford Transit or Mercedes Sprinter)**:
  - Driver completes daily restocking route in a 4-hour morning shift.
  - Pods use quick-swap rolling shelf totes for 15-minute replenishment per location.
- **Central Telemetry Dashboard**:
  - Real-time cloud monitoring of temperature, inventory levels, robotic cycle health, and door cycle counts.
  - Automatic re-order alerts triggered when any SKU drops below 15% stock.
